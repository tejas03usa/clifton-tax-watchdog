### 2. Public Records & Government Data Disclaimer
MyTownLens aggregates and visualizes public records collected from official government bodies in New Jersey, including:
* County Boards of Taxation (Abstracts of Ratables, MOD-IV Assessment Rolls)
* Municipal Tax Assessors and Collectors
* New Jersey Division of Local Government Services (DLGS / User-Friendly Budgets)
* New Jersey Department of Education (DOE / School District User-Friendly Budgets)
* New Jersey State Comptroller (OSC)
* County and Municipal Clerks (Petitions and Election Certifications)
* Public Employee Payroll Disclosures (N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1, Open Public Records Act)

**Public Domain Notice:** Raw government facts, statutory tax rates, certified municipal and school district budgets, public employee payroll records, and statutory election filings are public records.

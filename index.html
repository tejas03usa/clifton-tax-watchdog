Why is your assessed value so different from what your house sells for?

Like many New Jersey municipalities that haven't conducted a citywide revaluation in years, Clifton operates on an Equalization Ratio of ~33.3% (established by the Passaic County Board of Taxation). This means a home with a true market value of $600,000 is typically assessed on city books for approximately $200,000.

To compensate for these lower assessed values, the statutory tax rate appears higher (6.527%). When multiplied together: $200,000 × 6.527% = $13,054 annual tax, which equals an effective market tax rate of approximately 2.18% of true market value.

The Equalization Illusion: Clifton's certified rate (6.527%) looks more than double Nutley's (2.844%) or Montclair's (3.766%) only because Clifton's property assessments are frozen at ~33.3% of market value. NJ State Financial Disclosure Statements (FDS)

Outside Jobs, Businesses, LLCs & Gifts

Under N.J.S.A. 40A:9-22.1, every municipal leader and department head must file an annual FDS disclosing businesses, employers, and secondary real estate:

  1. Go to the State FDS Portal (fds.nj.gov) ↗.
  2. In the Filing Type menu, select "Local Government Officers".
  3. In County, select Passaic; in Local Government Entity, select Clifton City.
  4. Leave the name blank to see all filed disclosures for Clifton's Mayor, City Council, City Manager, CFO, Tax Assessor, and Planning Board.
  5. For School Board Trustees & Superintendent, visit the School Ethics FDS Portal ↗.

NJ Business Gateway & Corporate Filings

LLCs, Corporations & Trade Names

To check if a city official, teacher, police officer, or vendor operates an active LLC or business entity in New Jersey:

  1. Open the NJ Division of Revenue Business Name Search ↗.
  2. Enter the business name or trade name keyword (e.g. Grabowski, Clifton).
  3. To find businesses owned by an individual, search by "Registered Agent" or "Officer/Director".
  4. You can view formation certificates, annual reports, dissolution status, and business mailing addresses.

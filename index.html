📐 Mandatory 2026–2027 Property Revaluation (ASI)

1st Since 1990

Ordered by the Passaic County Board of Taxation and NJ Division of Taxation, Clifton contracted Appraisal Systems, Inc. (ASI) for a $3.2M citywide revaluation of all ~24,745 properties.

  • Valuation Date: Assessed at 100% true market value as of October 1, 2026 (replacing frozen 1990 baselines of ~31.8%–33.3%).
  • Tax Bill Impact (2027): Revaluation is revenue-neutral in total levy—as assessments rise ~3× to market value, the nominal tax rate drops proportionately from 6.527% to ~2.2%–2.6%.
  • Extended Tax Appeal Deadline: Because of the revaluation, the formal Passaic County tax appeal deadline is extended from April 1 to May 1, 2027 (after ASI informal taxpayer reviews).

🛡️ Direct NJ Tax Relief: Stay NJ, ANCHOR & Freeze

Combined Form PAS-1

New Jersey now uses a single combined application (Form PAS-1) for seniors (65+) and Social Security disability recipients, alongside automated filing for general homeowners and renters:

  • Stay NJ (Seniors 65+): Rebates up to 50% of property taxes (capped up to $6,500/yr) for senior homeowners with household income under $500,000.
  • ANCHOR Rebate: Provides $1,000 to $1,750 for eligible homeowners (income ≤ $250,000) and $450 to $700 for Clifton renters (income ≤ $150,000).
  • Senior Freeze (PTR): Reimburses eligible seniors/disabled homeowners for any property tax increases above their established base year.
  • Filing Deadline: Submit combined Form PAS-1 by November 2, 2026.

Why is your assessed value so different from what your house sells for?

Like many New Jersey municipalities that haven't conducted a citywide revaluation in years, Clifton operates on an Equalization Ratio of ~33.3% (established by the Passaic County Board of Taxation). This means a home with a true market value of $600,000 is typically assessed on city books for approximately $200,000.

To compensate for these lower assessed values, the statutory tax rate appears higher (6.527%). When multiplied together: $200,000 × 6.527% = $13,054 annual tax, which equals an effective market tax rate of approximately 2.18% of true market value.

1. The 30-Year Shift: How Homeowners Became 76% of the Tax Base

Under New Jersey law, the total municipal, school, and county tax levy is divided across the town's net valuation. As commercial and industrial ratables declined or won tax appeals, residential homeowners were forced to pick up the slack.

📉
Commercial Tax Appeal Clawbacks

Corporate retail centers, big-box stores, and office parks regularly sue the City in NJ Tax Court. Over the past decade, Clifton paid over $22.5 Million in appeal refunds and reduced commercial assessments permanently.

🏭
Industrial to Residential Conversion

Former manufacturing plants and research campuses (Hoffmann-La Roche, historic Botany mills) closed or converted into multi-family housing, replacing heavy taxpayers with student-generating residences.

🏛️
The Assessment Equalization Lag

Without a periodic citywide revaluation, rising residential home values outpaced commercial assessments, silently shifting a higher mathematical ratio of the county and school levy onto homeowners.

How to Use Official State & County Public Portals

Step-by-Step Instructions
🏡

Passaic County MOD-IV & Cit-e-Net Tax Portals

Real Estate & Assessment History

To search outside properties or verify assessed values without knowing an exact spelling:

  1. Open City Cit-e-Net: Click the direct City Tax Portal ↗. You can search by Street Name only (leave number blank to see everyone on that block).
  2. Countywide Search: Visit the Passaic County Board of Taxation ↗. Under District, select 1602 (Clifton City).
  3. Search Partial Names: In the County MOD-IV engine, entering just the first 4 letters of a last name (e.g. GRAB) returns all matching deeds and corporate trusts across the county.
Open Official Clifton Cit-e-Net Lookup ↗
📋

NJ State Financial Disclosure Statements (FDS)

Outside Jobs, Businesses, LLCs & Gifts

Under N.J.S.A. 40A:9-22.1, every municipal leader and department head must file an annual FDS disclosing businesses, employers, and secondary real estate:

  1. Go to the State FDS Portal (fds.nj.gov) ↗.
  2. In the Filing Type menu, select "Local Government Officers".
  3. In County, select Passaic; in Local Government Entity, select Clifton City.
  4. Leave the name blank to see all filed disclosures for Clifton's Mayor, City Council, City Manager, CFO, Tax Assessor, and Planning Board.
  5. For School Board Trustees & Superintendent, visit the School Ethics FDS Portal ↗.
Visit NJ State FDS Portal (fds.nj.gov) ↗
🏢

NJ Business Gateway & Corporate Filings

LLCs, Corporations & Trade Names

To check if a city official, teacher, police officer, or vendor operates an active LLC or business entity in New Jersey:

  1. Open the NJ Division of Revenue Business Name Search ↗.
  2. Enter the business name or trade name keyword (e.g. Grabowski, Clifton).
  3. To find businesses owned by an individual, search by "Registered Agent" or "Officer/Director".
  4. You can view formation certificates, annual reports, dissolution status, and business mailing addresses.

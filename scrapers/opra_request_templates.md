# Clifton NJ Public Records (OPRA) Request Templates

Under the **New Jersey Open Public Records Act (N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1 et seq.)**, government records are public unless specifically exempted. Government agencies in New Jersey must respond within **seven (7) business days**.

## Template 1: Municipal Employee Overtime & Secondary Employment Disclosure

**Subject**: OPRA Request – Municipal Staff Overtime, Off-Duty Pay, and Secondary Employment Authorizations

> **To the Custodian of Records, City of Clifton:**
> 
> Pursuant to the New Jersey Open Public Records Act (N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1 et seq.) and the common law right to know, I am requesting access to and copies of the following government records:
> 
> 1. The complete payroll and compensation ledger for all City of Clifton employees for the most recent completed fiscal year, showing:
>    - Employee Name
>    - Department and Job Title
>    - Base Salary
>    - Overtime Compensation
>    - Longevity Pay, Stipends, and Other Cash Earnings
> 2. All approved Secondary Employment / Outside Employment Notification and Authorization forms filed by City of Clifton sworn police officers, department heads, and municipal staff currently active for the current calendar year.
> 3. The annual summary of Outside Police Employment Details (private contractor traffic details) showing vendors that contracted off-duty officers, hourly administrative fees collected by the City, and total amounts disbursed to participating officers.
> 
> As provided by N.J.S.A. 47:1A-5(e), employee names, titles, position, salary, payroll records, and dates of service are explicitly public records and cannot be redacted.
> 
> Please provide these records in electronic format (Excel or CSV preferred, or searchable PDF).
> 
> Thank you,  
> [Your Name]  
> [Your Clifton Address]  
> [Your Email & Phone Number]

## Template 2: Clifton Board of Education Vendor Invoices & Purchase Orders

**Subject**: OPRA Request – Clifton BOE Accounts Payable, Professional Service Contracts, and Warrant Lists

> **To the Business Administrator / Board Secretary, Clifton Board of Education:**
> 
> Pursuant to N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1 et seq., I hereby request electronic copies of the following public records:
> 
> 1. The complete Accounts Payable / Check Register / Bill List approved by the Board of Education for the past 12 months, detailing Vendor Name, PO Number, Budget Account Line Code, Check Date, Check Amount, and Brief Description of Services.
> 2. The contracts, agreements, and awarded fee schedules for all Professional Services contracts awarded for the current school year, including:
>    - Board Attorney / General Legal Counsel and Special Counsel
>    - District Auditing and Accounting Firms
>    - Architects of Record and Engineering Consultants
>    - Health Insurance Broker of Record
> 3. All Business Entity Disclosure Certifications (Pay-to-Play certifications and Chapter 271 Political Contribution Disclosures) submitted by vendors awarded contracts in excess of $17,500 during the current school year.
> 
> Please furnish these records electronically via email.
> 
> Thank you,  
> [Your Name]  
> [Your Clifton Address]  
> [Your Email & Phone Number]

## Template 3: Administrator Contracts, Buyouts & Perks

> 
> Pursuant to N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1 et seq., please provide copies of:
> 
> 1. The current employment contracts and any amendments for:
>    - Superintendent of Schools
>    - Assistant Superintendent for Business / Board Secretary
>    - Assistant Superintendent of Curriculum & Instruction
> 2. The official calculation of accrued unused sick leave, unused vacation leave, and contractual payout eligibility for the top 5 highest compensated district administrators.
> 3. Any car allowances, travel reimbursement schedules, or executive stipend authorizations approved by the Board of Education for the current fiscal year.
> 
> Under N.J.S.A. 47:1A-10, public officer employment contracts and compensation terms are open public records.
